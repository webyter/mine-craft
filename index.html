How to Play

MoveDrag the joystick on the left. Push it all the way to sprint.
LookDrag anywhere on the right side of the screen.
Break / AttackHold your finger on a block to mine it. Tap a mob to hit it.
Place / UseTap a block face to place the item in your hand, open chests, tables and furnaces, or eat food.
Jump / FlyTap jump. In Creative, double-tap jump to fly; use ▲ / ▼ to rise and sink.
SneakTap the sneak button to crouch so you won't fall off edges.
InventoryTap ••• next to the hotbar. Tap a slot to pick up a stack, tap another to drop it. Hold to split.
CraftingPut materials in the 2×2 grid (or 3×3 at a Crafting Table). Logs → planks → sticks → tools.
CommandsTap the chat button and type /help — try /time set night, /gamemode creative, /give diamond 64.
KeyboardWASD move, Space jump, Shift sneak, Ctrl sprint, E inventory, T chat, 1–9 hotbar, F fly (creative). Drag to look, hold to mine, right-click to place.
